Research

Sherlocking by Market-Making Middlemen

Work in Progress

I study how platforms choose between private-label imitation and wholesale contracts in the context of “Sherlocking,” where marketplace data are used to identify high-demand products. I examine how this choice affects competition and welfare.

Price Equilibrium with Selling Constraints

Work in Progress

with José-Luis Moraga-González and Makoto Watanabe

We study price competition in markets with selling constraints, where firms cannot attend to all buyers who want to inspect their products. We show how these constraints can lead to inefficiently high equilibrium prices.
